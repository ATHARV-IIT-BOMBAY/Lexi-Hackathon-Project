## 1. The Emotional Hook & The Real Problem
**[Action]** Start on a browser tab showing a generic article about Dyslexia struggles, or just a blank screen with a powerful quote. 

**[Audio]**
> "Hi judges, I am Atharv. Imagine being a highly intelligent 7-year-old child, but every time you are asked to write a simple sentence, your hand cramps, the letters float around the page, and you feel like a failure. Dyslexia affects 1 in 5 people globally. But as I began researching this space, I discovered a massive, overlooked crisis."

**[Action]** Switch tabs to show a medical article or Wikipedia page on **Dysgraphia**. Highlight the word "Dysgraphia".

**[Audio]**
> "While researching, I found that up to 30% of children with dyslexia also suffer from **Dysgraphia**—a neurological condition that makes the physical act of handwriting incredibly difficult. 
> 
> If you look at the AI and accessibility tools available today, they are severely limited. They all focus exclusively on *reading*. They offer text-to-speech, they change the background colors, or they use special fonts. But none of them actually teach a dyslexic child *how to write*. They ignore the physical mechanics entirely. 
> 
> I realized that to truly solve this, I had to go crazy. I had to build a clinical-grade occupational therapy tool right in the browser. Enter: Lexi."

## 2. Introducing The Solution
**[Action]** Switch to the Lexi Splash Screen, wait 2 seconds for it to transition to the Landing Page.

**[Audio]**
> "Lexi is a multi-sensory, gamified handwriting therapy platform. It isn't just a drawing app; it is heavily rooted in the **Orton-Gillingham methodology**—the gold-standard clinical approach that builds neural pathways by engaging visual, auditory, and kinesthetic touch simultaneously."

## 3. Entering the App & Audio Feedback
**[Action]** Click "Try Live Demo", landing on the Main App interface. Let Lexi give the initial greeting.

**[Audio]**
> "When a student logs in, they are immediately greeted by Lexi's interactive voice engine, which utilizes the Web Audio API and Speech Synthesis to act as a real-time, encouraging coach. This fulfills the auditory requirement of the Orton-Gillingham method."

## 4. A Comprehensive Curriculum
**[Action]** Briefly click through the bottom navigation tabs: Letters, Words, Numbers, and Shapes. Pause briefly on each to show the variety of the curriculum.

**[Audio]**
> "Lexi isn't just for practicing the alphabet. I built a comprehensive curriculum that includes uppercase and lowercase Letters, full Words, Numbers, and even Shapes to help develop foundational fine motor skills. Every single module uses the same underlying clinical engine to guide the student."

---

## 5. The Core Tech: Real-Time Vector Feedback
**[Action]** Go to the "Words" mode. Begin tracing the word slowly. First, trace perfectly on the path to show the rainbow effect. Then, intentionally stray off the path to show the red error ink. 

**[Audio]**
> "Let’s look at the actual tracing. I couldn't use standard off-the-shelf drawing libraries because I needed absolute precision, so I built a **Custom Canvas Vector Engine** from scratch. 
>
> As I draw, the engine acts like a virtual occupational therapist. If a child is tracing on the right track, they are rewarded with a beautiful, sparkling rainbow effect. But if their hand slips and they go off the route, the ink instantly turns red. This provides immediate, intuitive micro-corrections—keeping the child highly engaged without being punishing."

---

## 6. The AI Magic & Clinical Metrics
**[Action]** Finish tracing the word, then click Lexi at the top of the screen to trigger the AI Analysis. Show the modal with the score.

**[Audio]**
> "Here is where the platform gets really deep. Once I finish, Lexi analyzes my writing. But this isn’t a simple pass or fail. 
> 
> My client-side AI calculates **13 different clinical metrics** instantly. It looks at geometric accuracy, motor planning, start-point adherence, and even tremor detection. Usually, analyzing these biomechanics requires an expensive therapist watching the child write. I automated it using math. 
> 
> And because it runs entirely on the client side, it's lightning fast, perfectly private, and requires zero external API calls."

---

## 7. Gamification & Store
**[Action]** Close the score modal. Click on the Coins/Badge in the top right to open the Reward Store. Buy a color if you have enough coins.

**[Audio]**
> "Therapy is only effective if a child actually wants to do it. So I built a full gamification loop. High scores and accuracy earn coins, which kids can spend in my Reward Store to unlock new trace colors or buy gifts for Lexi."

## 8. The Dashboard & Raw Data
**[Action]** Close the store. Click the top right profile badge to open the Student Portal. Scroll through the charts slowly, pointing out the Tremor line going down.

**[Audio]**
> "All of this telemetry is useless if parents and teachers can't track it. Because I save everything offline using local storage persistence, I generate a real-time clinical dashboard. 
>
> Here in the Student Portal, you can see a 9-day active streak. If you look closely at the chart, you can actually watch my 'Tremor' metric steadily decreasing over the last 8 days as my motor control improves. This gives educators tangible, raw data to prove the therapy is working."
